06FAQ

Questions everyone asks.

How long does it take?

A landing page usually takes a few days. A business site takes one to three weeks, depending on how fast the text and photos arrive. A premium site depends on the scope and is agreed up front.

Who owns the domain and the site?

You do. The domain is registered in your name and the first year is included. Once fully paid, the site is yours.

I don't have photos or text. Is that a problem?

No. We'll help write the copy and choose photos. Real photos of your business always work best — we'll explain how to take them on your phone.

Do I have to take monthly care?

No. Without it the site is handed over to you, you renew the domain yourself (about ₪60–100 a year), and each change is paid separately.

Can I change things after launch?

Yes. With monthly care, small updates are included. Without it, each change has a fixed price agreed in advance.

How do I pay?

50% when we start and 50% when the site is ready and you're happy with it.
